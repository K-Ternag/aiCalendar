Short product description

Kids Calendar helps parents turn school notices into their children's schedules. It is a native Android calendar app for organizing school events, field trips, parent-teacher meetings and things to bring to school. Parents can import school notice photos, paste or share teacher and parent messages, or select original KakaoTalk notifications. The app prepares editable event drafts with dates, times, places and supplies stated in the notice. Parents review the details, choose the events relevant to their child and explicitly save them. Checklists, sharing cards, daily and weekly event views, and home screen widgets help families prepare for school days with less repeated typing.

Why Sign in with ChatGPT

School information often arrives as a photographed notice or a short message, leaving parents to copy dates and remember supplies themselves. We want to connect a parent's own ChatGPT account to that school-planning workflow. The requested integration would let eligible users connect inside the Android app and, subject to OpenAI approval and granted permissions, use their ChatGPT plan for user-initiated text and image analysis. A parent could select a field-trip notice and receive an editable draft containing the event time and the packed lunch or water bottle listed in the notice. When a notice contains several events, parents review the candidates and save only the relevant ones. Additional preparation suggestions are requested only when the parent asks for them. Photos are sent as images and text as the selected original input.

Permission and user control

The intended users are parents and guardians managing their children's school schedules. We request review of both account authentication and ChatGPT plan-based AI inference permissions for the Android app. Authentication approval alone is not represented as inference approval. Parents sign in through the system browser and review data transfer and possible plan usage before the first connection. The app does not ask for a ChatGPT password or an API key. Every event must be reviewed and explicitly saved by the user. Notification collection and filtering do not invoke AI or send notification contents to OpenAI; parents choose the school-related original message to analyze. Users can disconnect ChatGPT and continue managing schedules with manual entry and basic autofill without AI.

Website role

The public website is a static, bilingual product introduction for review. It presents school notices, children's events and school-day preparation as the main use case, alongside the Android experience, intended ChatGPT integration and data handling. It uses actual app screenshots with synthetic example data; some screenshots show general example events. It does not provide web sign-in, inference, event storage or an OAuth callback endpoint.
